The live face follows one interactive Codex CLI session: blue with a moving dot means working, amber means Codex is blocked on you, white means ready, and gray means its state is unknown or more than one session matches. The bar lights when your keystrokes would reach that exact session.

Tip: hold the key for ½ second while Codex is frontmost to capture its project and session. Press to raise its iTerm2 or Terminal window and, under tmux, select its exact pane.

Requires: Automation access for iTerm2 and/or Terminal for the hot indicator, capture, and focusing (macOS prompts on first use). Process and working-state detection itself needs no permission. Codex Desktop and non-interactive codex exec jobs are not targets.
